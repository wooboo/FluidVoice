# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who dictate frequently on Android and macOS and want private, desktop-powered transcription available inside any editable app without changing keyboards.

## Product Purpose

FluidVoice turns speech into polished text using a paired desktop. Success means starting a dictation from the current app, understanding its state at a glance, and receiving text in the focused field with minimal interruption.

## Brand Personality

Focused, capable, discreet. FluidVoice should feel calm at rest and precise while working, with its identity expressed through the folded F mark rather than decorative chrome.

## Anti-references

Do not imitate chat-head menus, persistent toolbars, playful assistant mascots, or overlays that cover content. Do not require users to understand networking terminology during normal dictation.

## Design Principles

- Stay out of the way until invoked.
- Make recording and destructive actions unmistakable.
- Prefer immediate feedback over decorative motion.
- Preserve user control before text is committed.
- Fall back safely when an app cannot accept inserted text.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use Android-standard accessibility semantics, touch targets of at least 48dp, state cues that do not rely on color alone, concise content descriptions, haptic feedback, and motion that remains understandable when system animations are reduced.
